The code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular classification task (HAR dataset). MultiModalPredictor is designed for text, image, and tabular data fusion and is significantly heavier and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Given the 10-fold cross-validation requirement and the strict time constraints (110s per fold), `MultiModalPredictor` is likely to time out or fail to converge within the allocated resources. Furthermore, the `__del__` method attempting to remove the model directory while the predictor might still be active or locked by the OS can lead to runtime errors.
