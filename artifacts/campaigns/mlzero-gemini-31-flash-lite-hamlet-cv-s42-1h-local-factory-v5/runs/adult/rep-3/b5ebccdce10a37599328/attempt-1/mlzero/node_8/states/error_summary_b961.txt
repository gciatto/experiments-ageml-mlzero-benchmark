The provided code uses `TabularPredictor(..., path=self.path)` inside a factory function that is called repeatedly during cross-validation. Because `AutoGluon` creates persistent directories on disk, and the `AutoGluonWrapper` attempts to clean up this directory in `__del__`, there is a high risk of race conditions, file locking issues, or premature deletion of artifacts while the predictor is still being accessed or during the cross-validation loop. Furthermore, the `time_limit=100` is quite aggressive for the 'extreme' preset, which may lead to incomplete model training or inconsistent performance across folds.
