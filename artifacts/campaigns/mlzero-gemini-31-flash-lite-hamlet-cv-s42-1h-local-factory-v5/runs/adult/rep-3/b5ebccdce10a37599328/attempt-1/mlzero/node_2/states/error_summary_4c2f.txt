The provided code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a tabular classification task. While AutoGluon is powerful, the `MultiModalPredictor` is primarily designed for deep learning-based multimodal tasks (text, image, tabular) and is significantly more resource-intensive and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Given the strict time constraints of a ten-fold cross-validation benchmark (3530s total, ~353s per fold), the `MultiModalPredictor` is likely to exceed the time limit or fail due to overhead. Furthermore, the `__del__` method attempting to `shutil.rmtree` the model directory is prone to race conditions or permission errors in some environments. It is recommended to use `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor` for this specific dataset.
