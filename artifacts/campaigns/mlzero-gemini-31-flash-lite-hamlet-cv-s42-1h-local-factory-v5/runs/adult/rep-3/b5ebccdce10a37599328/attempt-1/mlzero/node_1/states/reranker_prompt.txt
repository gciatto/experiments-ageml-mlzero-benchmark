The data science task is a **classification** problem based on the "adult" dataset.

*   **Problem Type:** Classification.
*   **Evaluation Metric:** `balanced_accuracy` (higher is better).
*   **Input Format:** A CSV file (`data.csv`) containing the following columns: `age`, `workclass`, `fnlwgt`, `education`, `education-num`, `marital-status`, `occupation`, `relationship`, `race`, `sex`, `capital-gain`, `capital-loss`, `hours-per-week`, `native-country`, and `class`.
