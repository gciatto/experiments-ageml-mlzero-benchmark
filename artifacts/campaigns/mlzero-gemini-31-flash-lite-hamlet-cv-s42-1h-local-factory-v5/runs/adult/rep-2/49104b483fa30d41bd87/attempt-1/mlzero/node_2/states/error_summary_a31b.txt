The code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular classification task on the "adult" dataset. MultiModalPredictor is designed for text, image, and tabular data fusion and is significantly more resource-intensive and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Given the 10-fold cross-validation requirement and the constraints of the benchmark environment, using the MultiModal variant is likely to lead to timeouts or resource exhaustion. Furthermore, the `AutoGluonWrapper` creates a new directory for every fold, but the cleanup logic (`shutil.rmtree`) inside `__del__` is unreliable in Python due to garbage collection timing, which may lead to disk space issues during the 10-fold execution.
