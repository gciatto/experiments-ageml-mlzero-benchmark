The provided code uses `__del__` to clean up AutoGluon artifacts. In many Python environments, `__del__` is unreliable and may trigger during or after the `predict` method is called, potentially deleting the model directory while the predictor is still trying to access it. Furthermore, AutoGluon's `fit` method creates persistent files; relying on `shutil.rmtree` in `__del__` is prone to permission errors or race conditions. Additionally, the `time_limit` of 300s per fold is quite aggressive for the 'extreme' preset, which may lead to incomplete training or timeouts in a 10-fold CV setting.
