The provided code uses `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor` which, by default, creates persistent disk-based artifacts. While the wrapper attempts to clean up using `shutil.rmtree` in `__del__`, AutoGluon's internal processes (especially with `best_quality` presets) often spawn background processes or maintain file locks that prevent successful cleanup of the temporary directories. In a cross-validation loop, this leads to rapid disk space exhaustion and potential file permission/locking errors. Furthermore, the `time_limit` of 240s per fold is likely too aggressive for the `best_quality` preset, which triggers heavy ensemble and stacking logic that may exceed the total allocated time for the benchmark.
