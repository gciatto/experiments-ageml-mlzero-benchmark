The provided solution uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular regression task on the `auto_mpg` dataset. While `MultiModalPredictor` can handle tabular data, it is significantly heavier and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`, which is the standard and recommended tool for this type of dataset. Furthermore, the `__del__` method in the wrapper attempts to remove the model directory, which can lead to race conditions or errors if the predictor is still accessing files or if the cleanup fails during the cross-validation loop. Additionally, the `time_limit` of 280s per fold is likely to cause the process to exceed the total time budget for 10 folds (2800s + overhead), potentially leading to timeouts in the evaluation harness.
