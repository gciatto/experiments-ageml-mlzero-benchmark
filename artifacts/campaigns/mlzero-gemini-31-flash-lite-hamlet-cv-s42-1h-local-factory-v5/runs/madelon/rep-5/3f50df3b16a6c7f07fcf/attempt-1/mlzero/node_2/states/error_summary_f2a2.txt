The provided code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular classification task (Madelon dataset). MultiModalPredictor is designed for text, image, and tabular data fusion and is significantly heavier and less efficient for pure tabular data than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Furthermore, the `__del__` method in the wrapper is unreliable for cleaning up AutoGluon artifacts, as it may be called prematurely or fail to execute during process termination, potentially leading to disk space exhaustion during a 10-fold cross-validation run. Finally, the `time_limit` of 250s per fold is likely too aggressive for the MultiModalPredictor's overhead, which may lead to timeouts or poor performance compared to the TabularPredictor.
