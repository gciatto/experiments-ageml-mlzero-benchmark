The candidate used `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular classification task (Madelon dataset). MultiModalPredictor is designed for text, image, and tabular data fusion and is significantly heavier and less efficient for pure tabular data than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Furthermore, the `MultiModalPredictor` often requires specific column types (e.g., text or categorical) and may fail or perform poorly on the raw numerical features of the Madelon dataset. Additionally, the `__del__` method in the wrapper is unreliable for cleaning up AutoGluon artifacts, which can lead to disk space exhaustion during ten-fold cross-validation.
