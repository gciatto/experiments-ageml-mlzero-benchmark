The provided code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular regression task. While AutoGluon is permitted, the `MultiModalPredictor` is designed for unstructured data (text, images, etc.) and is significantly more resource-intensive and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Given the 10-fold cross-validation requirement and the 2721s total time budget, `MultiModalPredictor` is highly likely to exceed the time limit or fail due to resource constraints on standard tabular datasets like Ames Housing. Furthermore, the `__del__` method attempting to `shutil.rmtree` on the model directory is risky in a multi-process or parallelized CV environment, as it may delete files still in use or cause race conditions.
