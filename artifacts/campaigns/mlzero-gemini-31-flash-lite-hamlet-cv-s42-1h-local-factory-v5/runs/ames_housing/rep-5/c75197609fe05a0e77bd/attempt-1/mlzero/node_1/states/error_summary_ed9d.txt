The provided code uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular regression task. While AutoGluon is permitted, the `MultiModalPredictor` is designed for unstructured data (text, images) and is significantly more resource-intensive and slower than the `TabularPredictor` recommended for the Ames Housing dataset. Furthermore, the `__del__` method attempting to `shutil.rmtree` on the model directory is prone to race conditions or permission errors in multi-process cross-validation environments, which can lead to crashes during the evaluation of subsequent folds.
