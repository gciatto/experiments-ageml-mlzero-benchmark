The implementation uses `AutoGluon.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular regression task. MultiModalPredictor is designed for text/image/tabular fusion and is significantly heavier and slower than `AutoGluon.TabularPredictor`. Given the strict time constraints of 10-fold cross-validation (336s total, ~33s per fold), the overhead of initializing and running the MultiModal engine will likely lead to timeouts or resource exhaustion. Furthermore, the `__del__` method attempting to `shutil.rmtree` on the model directory is prone to race conditions or permission errors in distributed/parallel environments.
