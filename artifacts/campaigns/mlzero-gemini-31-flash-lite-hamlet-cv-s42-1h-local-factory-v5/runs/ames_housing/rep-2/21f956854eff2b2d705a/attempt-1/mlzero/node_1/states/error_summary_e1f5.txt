The implementation uses `autogluon.multimodal.MultiModalPredictor` for a standard tabular regression task. While AutoGluon is permitted, the `MultiModalPredictor` is designed for unstructured data (text, images) and is significantly more resource-intensive and slower than `autogluon.tabular.TabularPredictor`. Given the 10-fold cross-validation requirement and the time constraints of the benchmark, using the MultiModal variant is inefficient and likely to lead to timeouts or resource exhaustion compared to the appropriate `TabularPredictor`. Additionally, the `__del__` method for cleanup is unreliable in Python and may cause issues with AutoGluon's internal file locks or directory management during cross-validation.
