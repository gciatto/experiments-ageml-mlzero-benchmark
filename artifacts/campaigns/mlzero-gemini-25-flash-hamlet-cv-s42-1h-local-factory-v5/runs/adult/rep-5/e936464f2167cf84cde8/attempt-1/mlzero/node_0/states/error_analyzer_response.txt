ERROR_SUMMARY: The `ValueError` indicates that the `random_seed` argument was incorrectly passed to the `TabularPredictor.fit()` method. AutoGluon's `TabularPredictor` expects the `random_seed` to be provided during its initialization, not during the `fit` call. The `fit` method's valid arguments do not include `random_seed`.
SUGGESTED_FIX: Remove the `random_seed` argument from the `predictor.fit()` method call. Instead, pass the `random_seed` directly to the `TabularPredictor` constructor when initializing `self.predictor` within the `AutoGluonTabularWrapper`'s `fit` method.
