**Problem Type:** Classification.

**Input Format:** Tabular data. Features (`X`) are provided as all columns from `data.csv` except the 'class' column. The target (`y`) is the 'class' column from `data.csv`. These are supplied to the model's `fit(X,y)` and `predict(X)` methods.

**Prediction Output Format:** The model's `predict(X)` method must return predicted class labels.
