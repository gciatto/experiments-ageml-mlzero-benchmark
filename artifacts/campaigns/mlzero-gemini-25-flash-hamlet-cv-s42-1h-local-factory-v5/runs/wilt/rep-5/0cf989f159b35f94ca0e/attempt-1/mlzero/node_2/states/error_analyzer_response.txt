ERROR_SUMMARY: The `TabularPredictor` constructor in AutoGluon version 1.6.3 does not accept a `random_seed` argument directly. This parameter was removed or changed in recent AutoGluon versions, leading to a `ValueError` when attempting to initialize the predictor with it.
SUGGESTED_FIX: Remove the `random_seed` argument from the `TabularPredictor` constructor call. To ensure reproducibility, import `set_seed` from `autogluon.core.utils.utils` and call `set_seed(RANDOM_SEED)` at the beginning of the `build_model` function or the `fit` method to apply the seed globally for AutoGluon operations.
